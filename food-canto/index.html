Our kitchen

Cooked at home. Started after you order.

FOOD CANTO began the way most good food does — one family kitchen, recipes passed down by hand, and neighbours asking for "just one more tray". We still cook exactly that way.

We take orders in advance, buy ingredients for your date, and cook in small batches with the kind of attention only a home kitchen can afford. Fresh isn't a claim here; it's the schedule.

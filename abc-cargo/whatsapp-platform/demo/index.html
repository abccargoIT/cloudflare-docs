Meta rules the design obeys

not optional
24-hour service window After the customer's last message you may reply freely for 24 hours. Once it closes, only templates Meta has approved can go out. The composer in this demo locks itself when that happens.
Templates are pre-approved, and charged Shipment updates, delivery confirmations and document requests are utility templates. Customer-initiated conversations inside the window are free; template messages are billed per message at rates that vary by country.
Webhooks must be answered fast, and may repeat Meta expects an immediate acknowledgement and retries if it does not get one. Work is queued rather than done inline, and duplicates are dropped by message id.
Media links expire quickly Photos of damaged cartons and delivery paperwork must be pulled from Meta and stored in R2 on arrival, or they are lost.
Each number carries its own quality rating Blocks and reports cut the daily limit of business-initiated conversations, so notifications stay tied to real shipment events and honour opt-out.

Everything here runs in your browser against sample conversations. Region 2 and Region 3 are placeholders until the number details are confirmed in WhatsApp Manager, and the tracking reference format ABC0000000 is a stand-in for the real ABC Cargo format. No Meta or Cloudflare account is contacted by this page.
